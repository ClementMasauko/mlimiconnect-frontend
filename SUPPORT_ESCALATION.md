# Support escalation

- Level 1 handles guidance without requesting passwords, PINs or full payment details.
- Level 2 handles verification, order evidence and provider references using ticket and correlation IDs.
- Level 3 engineering receives redacted timestamp, route, app version and correlation ID only.
- Security/privacy, payment loss, harmful advice and account takeover escalate immediately to the incident commander.
- Verify identity through approved controls before refunds, account changes or exports.
- Targets: SEV-1 15 minutes, payment/security 30 minutes, standard cases one working day.
