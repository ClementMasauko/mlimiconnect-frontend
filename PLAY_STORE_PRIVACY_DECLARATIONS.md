# Play Store privacy declarations

Camera is used only when the user chooses to photograph listing, traceability, pest-diagnosis or delivery evidence. Foreground location is used only for delivery tracking, radius search or traceability location. Neither is used for advertising or background tracking.

Declare camera and precise/approximate location in Play Data Safety, link the production privacy policy, and provide deletion instructions. Manual entry remains available when permission is declined. Sentry receives no form bodies, credentials, full identity, photographs or coordinates.
