# Incident response

1. Acknowledge within 15 minutes and assign an incident commander.
2. Classify SEV-1 safety/payment/data exposure, SEV-2 major outage, or SEV-3 limited degradation.
3. Open an audited incident in Admin → Operations and publish a status update without personal data.
4. Contain it with provider/feature disablement, credential rotation and preservation of redacted correlation IDs.
5. Never put passwords, PINs, phone numbers, messages or financial data in incident records.
6. Update the status page every 30 minutes for SEV-1 and hourly for SEV-2.
7. Validate recovery with probes, reconciliation and backup-restoration verification when data is affected.
8. Resolve publicly and complete a blameless review within five working days.

Emergency contacts belong in the private operations directory, not this repository.
